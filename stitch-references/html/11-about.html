How the Non-Linear Model Works

Human respiratory tissue experiences compound physiological stress. As particulate saturation rises from Moderate (AQI 100-200) into Severe (AQI 400+), acute outpatient consults, nebulizer regimens, and emergency bed allocations compound parabolically rather than linearly.
